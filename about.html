Our Journey

Milestones In Dough

From a humble stone oven in 1989 to a cornerstone of local artisan baking.

🌾
1989

The First Loaf

Maria's grandmother opened a small neighbourhood bakehouse with a single stone hearth, crafting classic sourdoughs and rye loaves daily.

🥐
2005

Second Generation

Maria took over the bakery leadership, introducing traditional French viennoiserie and expanding the daily offering with delicate seasonal pastries.

🔥
2019

Custom Wood-Fired Hearth

Installed a custom-built wood-fired stone oven, bringing time-honored artisanal baking temperatures and deep crust character back to every loaf.

🤝
2024

Community Bakery Initiative

Launched our weekly "Pay-it-Forward" loaf program and community partnerships, donating over 150 fresh loaves each week to local food banks.
